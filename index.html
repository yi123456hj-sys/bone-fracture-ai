Data Pipeline

From Raw to Ready

A rigorous multi-stage pipeline ensures dataset quality and reproducibility.

📁
Step 01
Raw Collection
Original X-ray images sourced from peer-reviewed clinical papers, medical databases, and open-access repositories.
Raw Data
🔑
Step 02
Deduplication
MD5 hash matching eliminated exact duplicates. Perceptual hashing removed visually similar augmented pairs.
MD5 + pHash
⚡
Step 03
Augmentation
Controlled augmentation including rotations, flips, brightness and contrast variations to balance class distributions.
Augmentation
✂️
Step 04
Train / Test Split
Stratified random splitting into 80% training and 20% testing sets, with refined boundaries in the fixed dataset.
Stratified Split
